GIS Customer Service Zoning

Petaluma Zoning Lookup Tool

An interactive lookup tool that answers common zoning questions for residents and applicants, integrated with an ArcGIS Online map. Designed to reduce counter-level calls by providing clear definitions and spatial context without requiring planning staff intervention.

Active Use Lot Coverage Hercules

Hercules Lot Coverage Calculator

A calculator that helps applicants interpret the maximum lot coverage section of Hercules's zoning code — a notoriously complex provision. This tool is actively in use by the City of Hercules to support applicant self-service and reduce staff time spent on routine coverage calculations.

View tool
CEQA PSA Timelines

California PSA / CEQA Timeline Tool

An interactive timeline tool that maps the full permitting lifecycle under the California Permit Streamlining Act and CEQA. Clearly delineates which party — applicant or agency — is responsible at each stage, helping applicants understand their rights and obligations and reducing procedural disputes.

View tool
SmartCode Petaluma Entitlement

Petaluma Permitting Pathway Tool

An applicant-facing decision tree that guides users through Petaluma's permitting pathways under its SmartCode. Helps applicants determine whether their project is by-right, ministerially approved, or subject to discretionary review — reducing uncertainty at the earliest stage of the entitlement process.

View tool
AI-Assisted Staff Tool Correspondence

LetterGPT — Planning Letter Drafting Assistant

A staff-facing tool that accelerates planning letter drafting by gathering project-specific information through a structured interview, then assembling a professional prompt for use with any AI assistant. Multiple human review checkpoints ensure professional judgment and code interpretation remain in the planner's hands throughout the process.

GIS / AGOL EIR 300 MW BESS

Borealis BESS Project Web App

A public stakeholder web application built in ArcGIS Online to support a complex 300 MW battery energy storage facility in unincorporated Sonoma County — a project requiring tribal coordination and a full Environmental Impact Report. The app helps stakeholders understand the project's spatial context, scope, and environmental setting, supporting informed public participation.

About

I am a Planner at M-Group, where I serve as Consulting Planner to the City of Petaluma and support additional municipal clients. My work spans day-to-day zoning administration, state housing law implementation, CEQA compliance, and discretionary project entitlement.

Prior to M-Group, I worked at Permit Sonoma (Sonoma County), which gave me broad exposure to land use planning in both urban and rural contexts. I hold a BA in Sustainable Urban Development with a minor in GIS and am actively pursuing AICP certification.

Beyond traditional planning practice, I build web-based tools that make regulatory frameworks more accessible. I believe planning is most effective when it is transparent — when applicants can understand the rules, the timelines, and their own role in the process. That belief drives both my day-to-day practice and my side projects.
