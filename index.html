ArcPy Suitability Model Housing

Missing Middle Housing Suitability Analysis

A city-agnostic, ArcPy-driven framework for identifying parcels suited to Missing Middle Housing, applied to 10,666 residential parcels in Petaluma. The model screens out FEMA flood, CalFire fire hazard, and steep-slope areas derived from LiDAR, attaches adopted zoning development standards (IZO Chapter 4) to each parcel, and calculates volumetric development capacity to surface underbuilt and vacant candidate sites — so every result traces back to adopted regulation rather than external assumptions.

About

I am a Planner at M-Group, where I serve as Consulting Planner to the City of Petaluma and support additional municipal clients. My work spans day-to-day zoning administration, state housing law implementation, CEQA compliance, and discretionary project entitlement.

Prior to M-Group, I worked at Permit Sonoma (Sonoma County), which gave me broad exposure to land use planning in both urban and rural contexts. I hold a BA in Sustainable Urban Development with a minor in GIS and am actively pursuing AICP certification.

GIS is central to how I practice. I write ArcPy workflows that translate adopted zoning standards into parcel-level attributes, build suitability and capacity models for housing policy work, and publish ArcGIS Online web maps and Experience Builder applications for public engagement. Pairing the regulatory side of planning with reproducible spatial analysis means the maps and numbers behind a recommendation can always be traced back to the ordinance.

I also build web-based tools that make regulatory frameworks more accessible. I believe planning is most effective when it is transparent — when applicants can understand the rules, the timelines, and their own role in the process. That belief drives both my day-to-day practice and my side projects.
